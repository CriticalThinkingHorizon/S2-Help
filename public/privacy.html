Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

1. Summary

This Site is a static informational guide with a small, aggregated popularity counter for FAQ entries. It does not require an account, does not use advertising or third-party analytics, and does not set cookies or persistent identifiers in your browser.

2. Information we process

  • FAQ popularity events: when you open an FAQ entry, your browser sends the entry's identifier to the server over a WebSocket connection. No user identifier is sent or stored. Only aggregate per-entry counts are kept.
  • Server logs: like most web servers, the hosting infrastructure may process your IP address, user agent, requested path, and timestamps for security, abuse prevention, and basic operational purposes.

3. What we do not collect

  • No names, email addresses, or account details.
  • No cookies, localStorage, or sessionStorage identifiers.
  • No third-party analytics, advertising trackers, or social plugins.
  • No external font or asset requests (fonts are served from this Site or your system).

4. Purposes and legal bases

We process the limited data described above to operate the FAQ popularity feature (legitimate interests in providing and improving the Site) and to keep the Site secure and available (legitimate interests in security and abuse prevention). Where required by law, we rely on your consent or another lawful basis; you can object to processing based on legitimate interests as described below.

5. Retention

Aggregate FAQ counters are retained until they are reset or the Site is reconfigured. Server logs, if collected by the hosting provider, are retained according to that provider's policies.

6. Sharing and hosting

The Site is hosted on Wasmer Edge, which processes requests on our behalf as a hosting provider. The source code is hosted on GitHub. We do not sell personal data. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the Site, its users, or third parties.

7. International transfers

Hosting and source-code providers may process data in countries other than yours. Where required, transfers are covered by appropriate safeguards offered by those providers.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to the processing of your personal data, and to data portability or to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Because the Site does not identify individual visitors, we may be unable to locate data relating to you. To make a request, use the contact details below.

9. Children

The Site is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us so we can address it.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect the Site, including transport encryption when served over HTTPS and input validation. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated at any time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the date above.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, open an issue at the project repository.
